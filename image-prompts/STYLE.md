# T-shirt graphic style sheet

Print-ready T-shirt front graphics for a dark-humor dungeon-crawler game-show fandom. Each design is a single standalone graphic on a fully transparent background. It is not a shirt mockup: never draw a shirt, model, hanger or fabric. Design for screen printing on a black or charcoal shirt. Use bold, clean silhouettes, thick confident outlines and a limited palette of 3 to 5 flat inks per design. Halftone dots or a light distressed texture are allowed when the prompt asks for them. Shapes must stay readable at 10 inches wide and at a glance from across a room. All lettering must be spelled exactly as written in the prompt, with crisp, chunky, legible letterforms and no extra words. The composition is centered, with generous empty margin around the graphic and nothing cropped at the edges. Never include watermarks, signatures, copyright marks, photo backgrounds, gradients that fade into a background color, or real brand logos.

## Stickers (prompts that start with "A die-cut sticker")
These rules replace the shirt rules above. The graphic is a single die-cut vinyl sticker for laptops and water bottles, not a shirt print, so it may use any colours, not only ones that read on a black shirt. One thick, even, solid white border (about 3 percent of the image width) follows the outer silhouette of the whole graphic, with one smooth outline and no gaps or holes. Everything outside that white border is fully transparent. Square 1:1 canvas, the sticker centered and filling about 85 percent of it, nothing cropped. Bold shapes, thick dark outlines, flat colour with simple cel shading, few small details: it must read clearly at 3 inches wide. Lettering is short, chunky and spelled exactly as written. No drop shadow under the sticker, no peel corner, no mockup, no hand, no photo.
